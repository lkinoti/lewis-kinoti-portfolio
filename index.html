FAQ

Common questions

What kind of work do you take on?

Mostly ICT and digital: websites, technical support and networking, graphic design, video and photography, social media management, cybersecurity awareness and digital skills training. I also take on community and student media work.

Do you work remotely or on-site?

Both. I work remotely with clients anywhere, and on-site within Bondo and Siaya County. For events and training sessions outside the county, travel and accommodation are discussed upfront.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope, complexity and deadline. After a short conversation about what you need, I send a clear quote with deliverables and a timeline — no surprises later.

Do you offer training for groups or schools?

Yes. I run practical sessions on computer basics, Microsoft Office, Google Workspace, Canva, video editing, digital safety and online presence. Sessions are tailored to the group's level and goals.

Can you help improve my existing website?

Yes — redesigns, responsiveness fixes, performance work, content updates, hosting and domain setup, and ongoing maintenance. A short audit is usually enough to scope the work.

How quickly can you start?

Usually within a few days for small tasks, and one to two weeks for larger builds. Urgent deadlines are welcome — tell me the date and I'll confirm whether it's realistic.
